    Will this email hold up everywhere?

    Paste HTML, drop an .eml, or connect Mailpit, MailHog or Mailtrap. email-lens shows:

    • Client support for every HTML and CSS feature the email uses, across ~45 clients (caniemail.com data)
    • Device previews from 320px phones to desktop, in light, dark, forced-dark, stripped-CSS and images-off modes
    • Live engines: scroll and click the email in real WebKit (Apple Mail), Chromium (webmail, Android) and Gecko (Thunderbird)
    • Checks for overflow on phones, Gmail clipping, contrast, tap targets, alt text and more

    Each tile is a real engine running on this machine with scripts off, as in email clients: WebKit for Apple Mail and iOS Mail, Chromium for webmail and Android apps, Gecko for Thunderbird. Scroll, hover and click inside a tile; clicked links are shown, not followed. Nothing is saved: tiles close when you switch emails or leave the page, and Snapshot opens a full-length image in a new tab without storing it.
